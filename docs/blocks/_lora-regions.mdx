:::info
`EU_433` and `EU_868` have to adhere to an hourly duty cycle limitation of 10%, calculated every minute on a rolling 1-hour basis. Your device will stop transmitting if you reach it, until it is allowed again.

`EU_866` is duty cycle limited to 2.5% for mobile devices, or 10% when the device role is `ROUTER` or `ROUTER_LATE`.
:::

:::info EU sibling regions
`EU_868`, `EU_866` and `EU_N_868` cover the same harmonized European SRD allocation with different channel plans and modem presets, and each accepts its own exclusive set of presets. Selecting a preset that belongs to one of the siblings will automatically switch the region to that sibling.
:::

:::warning Amateur radio regions
The `ITU1_*`, `ITU2_*` and `ITU3_*` regions operate in amateur radio allocations and can only be selected if [`IsLicensed`](/docs/configuration/radio/user#is-licensed-ham) is enabled on the device. Review the [privileges and restrictions](/docs/faq/#amateur-radio-ham) of operating Meshtastic under an amateur license first — in particular, encryption is not permitted and your call sign must be transmitted.

The listed power limits are the regulatory ceiling used by the firmware, not a per-country limit — many administrations allow licensed operators to run well above 30 dBm, while some do not allocate the full range shown (for example 220 - 222 MHz in the USA and Canada, or parts of 420 - 450 MHz). Check your national bandplan before transmitting.
:::

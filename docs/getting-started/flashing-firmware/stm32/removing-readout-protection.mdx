title: Removing STM32 Readout Protection
sidebar_label: Removing Readout Protection
sidebar_position: 4
description: Clear flash readout protection (RDP level 1) on an STM32WL device so Meshtastic firmware can be flashed.
---

Some boards, such as the Wio-E5, ship with flash readout protection (RDP level 1)
enabled.
[STM32CubeProgrammer](https://www.st.com/en/development-tools/stm32cubeprog.html)
connects but cannot read, verify, or write flash. Removing the protection clears
it but erases the whole chip, including the factory firmware.

:::warning

Removing RDP resets the MCU. Connect **under reset** (`mode=UR`, or **Connect
Under Reset** in the GUI) so the programmer keeps control of the board afterward
&mdash; on a board without a BOOT0 pin there is no other way to reach it again.

:::

**GUI &mdash; STM32CubeProgrammer**

1. Connect over SWD, or over UART with the board in the
   [ROM bootloader](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/stm32/entering-bootloader-mode).
2. Choose **Read Unprotect (MCU)** from the target menu.
3. Wait for the mass erase to finish, then reconnect.
4. Flash the Meshtastic firmware as described in
   [Flashing over UART](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/stm32/flashing-over-uart)
   or [Flashing over SWD](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/stm32/flashing-over-swd).

**CLI**

STM32CubeProgrammer's `-rdu` (read unprotect) removes RDP and mass-erases the
chip. Over SWD:

```shell
STM32_Programmer_CLI -c port=SWD mode=UR -rdu
```

or over the UART bootloader:

```shell
STM32_Programmer_CLI -c port=/dev/ttyUSB0 br=115200 -rdu
```

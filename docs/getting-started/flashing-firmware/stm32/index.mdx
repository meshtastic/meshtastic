## Flashing Methods for STM32 Devices

STM32WL devices have a ROM bootloader that accepts firmware over UART or SWD.
Flashing has two steps: put the chip into its ROM bootloader, then write the
firmware. Meshtastic firmware for STM32WL targets is distributed as
`firmware-<target>-X.X.X.xxxxxxx.bin` and flashed to `0x08000000`; the `.elf` in
the same release also works with
[STM32CubeProgrammer](https://www.st.com/en/development-tools/stm32cubeprog.html).

For the BOOT0 pin location, UART pins, and any board-specific steps, see the
documentation for your board.

### Entering Bootloader Mode

STM32WL has no USB DFU mode. The
[bootloader can be entered](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/stm32/entering-bootloader-mode)
from a Meshtastic client, with the BOOT0 pin, or with an SWD programmer.

### Flashing from Web Flasher

:::info

Browser-based flashing is a work-in-progress and not available for STM32 targets yet.

:::

### Flashing over UART

A USB-TTL serial adapter can
[flash STM32WL over UART](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/stm32/flashing-over-uart)
without an SWD programmer, once the board is in bootloader mode.

### Flashing over SWD

An SWD programmer such as an ST-LINK
[flashes the board over the SWD pins](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/stm32/flashing-over-swd)
and does not need BOOT0 or the ROM bootloader UART.

### Flashing over USB

:::info

STM32WL has no USB peripheral, so there is no USB DFU mode. UART and SWD are the
only bootloader transports.

Any USB port connects to an internal USB-to-UART bridge and can be used for UART
flashing, unless that bridge is not wired to a bootloader UART (as on the
Wio-E5).

:::

### Removing Readout Protection

Some boards, such as the Wio-E5, ship with flash readout protection enabled.
STM32CubeProgrammer connects but cannot read or write flash until
[readout protection is removed](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/stm32/removing-readout-protection).

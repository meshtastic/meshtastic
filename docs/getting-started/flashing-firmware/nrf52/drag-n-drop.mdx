## Flash Firmware

:::info
You may now use the [Meshtastic Web Flasher](https://flasher.meshtastic.org) to download and copy firmware to your nRF52, RP2040, or RP2350-based devices. Alternatively, follow the instructions below to download and install firmware.
:::

If you have just performed a [Factory Erase](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/nrf52/nrf52-erase/), your device may automatically startup in bootloader mode, and you may skip to "Copy the appropriate firmware..." for your respective device below:

### nRF52

1. Download and unzip the latest firmware from [Meshtastic Downloads](https://meshtastic.org/downloads).
2. Connect your device to your computer with a USB data cable.
3. Put the device into [bootloader mode](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/nrf52/entering-bootloader-mode/). On most devices, double-press the reset button.
4. Notice a new drive will be mounted on your computer (Windows, Mac, Linux, or Android).
5. Open this drive and you should see three files: `CURRENT.UF2`, `INDEX.HTM`, and `INFO_UF2.TXT`.
6. Copy the appropriate firmware file (`firmware-DEVICE_NAME-X.X.X-xxxxxxx.uf2`) from the release onto this drive.
7. Once the file has finished copying onto the drive, the device will reboot and install the Meshtastic firmware.

#### What the bootloader checks

On the [Meshtastic nRF52 Bootloader](https://github.com/meshtastic/nRF52_Bootloader) (formerly OTAFIX) 2.6.0 and later:

- A firmware file built for the other SoftDevice version (the `SoftDevice` line in `INFO_UF2.TXT`) is rejected: the drive stays up, nothing is installed, and a board with a display shows WRONG FILE. Copy the right file next. Earlier bootloaders write such a file, and it can damage the SoftDevice.
- The old firmware is marked invalid as soon as the copy starts, so a cable pulled mid-copy leaves the device with no firmware rather than a damaged one. Plugged back into the computer, it comes back as the drive, showing NO FIRMWARE on boards with a display, and you copy the file again.
- Boards with a display show a progress bar while the file copies.

#### Backing up the installed firmware

`CURRENT.UF2` on the drive is a copy of the firmware installed on the device. Copy it to your computer to keep a backup, and copy it back onto the drive to reinstall that firmware. On OTAFIX 2.3-BP1.6 and later the copy is sized to the installed firmware; a copy taken with an older bootloader after a Bluetooth or serial update can be cut short, so take it again.

### RP2040
1. Download and unzip the latest firmware from [Meshtastic Downloads](https://meshtastic.org/downloads).
2. Press the BOOTSEL button and while keeping it pressed, connect the device to your computer via a USB cable.
3. Notice a new drive will be mounted on your computer (Windows, Mac, Linux, or Android).
4. Open this drive and you should see two files: `INDEX.HTM` and `INFO_UF2.TXT`.
5. Copy the appropriate firmware file (`firmware-DEVICE_NAME-X.X.X-xxxxxxx.uf2`) from the release onto this drive.
6. Once the file has finished copying onto the drive, the device will reboot and install the Meshtastic firmware.

### Potential Flashing Edge Cases

:::info

Before flashing confirm that you have [RAK4631](https://docs.rakwireless.com/product-categories/wisblock/rak4631/overview) and not a [RAK4631-R](https://docs.rakwireless.com/product-categories/wisblock/rak4631-r/overview) If this is not the case, fear not. The hardware is identical but requires [changing the bootloader](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/nrf52/convert-rak4631r).

:::

:::info

Previous versions of the Meshtastic firmware may save stale data, causing devices to get stuck in a crash loop during startup. If you experience issues when upgrading your nRF52 device from a previous version of Meshtastic, you may need to perform a full factory reset of the internal flash memory.

Follow the guide to [factory erase your nRF52](/docs/getting-started/flashing-firmware/nrf52/nrf52-erase) device before continuing to [flash firmware](#flash-firmware).

:::

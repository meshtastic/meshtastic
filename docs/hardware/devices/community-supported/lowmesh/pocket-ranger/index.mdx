[Pocket-Ranger](https://lowmesh.com/devices) and [Pocket-Scout](https://lowmesh.com/devices) nodes are injection molded weather resistant, perfect for a daily carry mesh communication device. Hardware and software is customized for long battery operated sessions by utilizing RX Duty Cycle and getting best-in-industry battery life.

Pocket-Ranger models have external antennas, while Pocket-Scouts have internal ones. PRO models have GPS, temperature, and humidity sensors.

## Specifications

- **MCU**
  - nRF52840 (Bluetooth 5 LE)
- **LoRa Transceiver**
  - Semtech SX1262
- **Frequency options**
  - 915 MHz
- **Antenna**
  - Whip antenna, 17CM +3dBi Gain (SMA connector)
- **Connectors**
  - USB-C
  - SMA
- **Battery**
  - 2000mAh LiPo with NTC Protection
- **Durability**
  - Dust & water resistant (IP rating pending)
  - IP67 USB-C
  - Waterproof SMA connector
  - ePTFE buzzer membrane

## Features

- User, Reset, and Power buttons
- Buzzer
- [Custom firmware](https://lowmesh.com/flasher), providing up to 4 weeks of runtime

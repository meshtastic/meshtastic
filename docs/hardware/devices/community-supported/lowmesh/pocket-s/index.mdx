Pocket-S is an outdoor solar node, perfect for a car dashboard or carrying with you hiking or camping. The built in 6.6v 0.8w solar panel + charging circuit can keep this device charged.

## Specifications

- **MCU**
  - nRF52840 (Bluetooth 5 LE)
- **LoRa Transceiver**
  - Semtech SX1262
- **Frequency options**
  - 915 MHz
- **Antenna**
  - Internal antenna
- **Connectors**
  - USB-C
- **Battery**
  - 2000mAh LiPo with NTC Protection

## Features

- Built-in solar panel (6.6v/0.8W)
- Reset and Power buttons
- Buzzer
- Custom firmware, providing up to 4 weeks of runtime

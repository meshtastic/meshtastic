[Pocket-M] is a MagSafe-compatible carry node that can easily connect to any MagSafe case or phone. Low profile and light weight, designed for ultra portability.

## Specifications

- **MCU**
  - nRF52840 (Bluetooth 5 LE)
- **LoRa Transceiver**
  - Semtech SX1262
- **Frequency options**
  - 915 MHz
- **Antenna**
  - Internal antenna
- **Connectors**
  - USB-C
- **Battery**
  - 1000mAh LiPo with NTC Protection

## Features

- MagSafe (for attachment only)
- Reset and Power buttons
- Buzzer
- [Custom firmware](https://lowmesh.com/flasher), providing up to 4 weeks of runtime

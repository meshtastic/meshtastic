Further information on the LILYGO® T-Echo devices can be found on LILYGO®'s [GitHub page](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LilyGO/LilyGO-T-Echo).

## T-Echo

<DeviceImage device="t-echo.svg" alt="LILYGO T-Echo" size="lg" center />

The T-Echo by LILYGO® supports a low power consumption micro-controller and an e-ink display.

### Specifications

- **MCU**
  - nRF52840 (Bluetooth BLE 5.0 & NFC)
- **LoRa Transceiver**
  - Semtech SX1262
- **Frequency options**
  - 433 MHz
  - 868 MHz
  - 915 MHz
- **Navigation Module**
  - L76K GNSS receiver (Supports GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS & QZSS)
- **Connectors**
  - U.FL antenna connector

### Features

- Reset, Program and capacitive touch buttons
- 1.54" eInk display
- Optional BME280 - Humidity and Pressure Sensor
- Comes with a case and battery

### Resources

- Firmware file: `firmware-t-echo-X.X.X.xxxxxxx.uf2`
- Purchase Links:

    - [Rokland 915MHz](https://msh.to/rokland-t-echo/)
    - [Rokland 915MHz w/ BME280 Kit](https://msh.to/rokland-t-echo-bme280/)
  - International
    - [LilyGO](https://msh.to/t-echo/)

## T-Echo Plus

<DeviceImage device="t-echo_plus.svg" alt="LILYGO T-Echo Plus" size="lg" center />

The T-Echo Plus keeps the T-Echo's nRF52840 and 1.54" e-ink display, and adds a 2400 mAh battery, a haptic vibration motor, a buzzer, and a 1/4-inch mount thread. It sells alongside the T-Echo rather than replacing it.

### Specifications

- **MCU**
  - nRF52840 (Bluetooth BLE 5.0 & NFC)
- **LoRa Transceiver**
  - Semtech SX1262
- **Frequency options**
  - 433 MHz
  - 868 MHz
  - 915 MHz
  - 920 MHz
- **Navigation Module**
  - L76K GNSS receiver (Supports GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS & QZSS)
- **Display**
  - 1.54" SPI e-ink, 200 x 200
- **Battery Capacity**
  - 2400 mAh
- **Motion Sensor**
  - BHI260AP IMU
- **Connectors**
  - U.FL antenna connector

### Features

- Two buttons plus a capacitive touch button.
- Haptic vibration motor and buzzer.
- 1/4-inch mount thread for a tripod or clamp.
- Optional BME280 temperature and pressure sensor.
- Supports [InkHUD](/docs/configuration/device-uis/inkhud/).

### Resources

- Firmware file: `firmware-t-echo-plus-X.X.X.xxxxxxx.uf2`
- Purchase Links:
  - International
    - [LilyGO](https://msh.to/t-echo-plus/)
